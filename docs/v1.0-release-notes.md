# BloomOS v1.0 — Release Notes

## What BloomOS v1.0 is

BloomOS is a full lifecycle platform for a luxury proposal/event studio: from first Lead contact through Proposal, Contract, Invoice, Payment, Client Journey, Operational Planning, Scheduling, Dispatch, Execution Package, Field Operations, Documents, and Completion — with Timeline, Knowledge Graph, Business Health, Executive Decisions, Reporting, Search, Notifications, Workflow Automation, a Client Portal, and Digital Asset Management running across all of it.

56 modules, 61 core domains, 171 routes, 125 permissions, and 8,639 passing tests make up this release. See `docs/v1.0-final-signoff.md` for the exact, computed statistics and the official production verdict.

## Highlights by area

**Commercial pipeline.** Lead capture and conversion, AI-assisted Proposal generation with a deterministic pricing engine, a full Contract Builder with clause/template libraries and e-signature integration, an Invoice Platform with line items/installments/credits, and a Payment ledger that reconciles against invoices automatically.

**Operations.** Client Journey stage tracking, Operational Planning (phases/milestones/deliverables/approvals), Scheduling (calendars, working hours, recurrence, capacity), Dispatch, Execution Packages (frozen on-the-day snapshots), Field Operations session tracking, Route Optimization, and a real-time Operations Center.

**Intelligence layer.** A Knowledge Graph connecting every entity in the system, a Business Health score, an Executive Decisions engine that scores and queues cross-platform recommendations from 17 platforms, a Reporting Platform with a custom Report Builder and Metric Registry, and Bloom AI — a context-aware Copilot embedded across every module.

**Workspace infrastructure.** Global Search & Command Center, unified Notifications, a visual Workflow Builder (drag-and-drop automation with 31 trigger types and 25+ action types), a Public API with OpenAPI 3.1 docs, Webhooks, a Marketplace, and a Digital Asset Management platform with folders, versioning, and Knowledge Graph-backed relationships.

**Client-facing.** A full Client Portal mirroring the commercial and operational lifecycle back to the client — Proposals, Contracts, Billing, Documents, Communication, Timeline, and Journey status — plus client onboarding that activates automatically the moment a deposit payment succeeds.

## This release's hardening work (Checkpoints 45, 45A, 45B)

Three back-to-back checkpoints closed every Critical and High severity finding from a full-repository security and architecture audit:

- **Checkpoint 45**: fixed 3 Critical cross-tenant data-exposure findings (Merge Field Engine, Internal Messaging, Digital Assets) and the systemic missing-permission-check gap across 13 operational platforms.
- **Checkpoint 45A — Production Hardening**: closed the remaining High severity findings — a missing permission tier for Executive Decisions/Objectives, 5 unguarded Medium-severity routes, Search coverage gaps, Reporting registry gaps, un-debounced search inputs, duplicate Analytics fetches, and eager Copilot/CommandPalette bundling.
- **Checkpoint 45B — Release Candidate & Production Sign-Off**: traced the entire event-delivery lifecycle end-to-end at the code level, confirmed 0 broken transitions, and ran the final full-repo quality gate.

**No Critical or High severity finding remains open as of this release.** The remaining Medium/Low items are documented, intentional deferrals — see `docs/v1.0-known-limitations.md`.

## Known limitations at v1.0

- Phase-1 MVP modules (Leads/Clients/Events/Vendors/Purchases/Inventory/Services/basic Contracts) enforce workspace isolation via Supabase RLS only, not granular role permissions server-side — a documented, intentional Phase-1 scope decision.
- The Workflow Action Registry and direct Notification wiring are thin on the 8 newest operational platforms; the generic Timeline→Workflow bridge compensates but isn't a bespoke integration.
- Field Operations has no code path into Document generation yet.
- Business Health's score predates the 5 newest operational platforms; Executive Decisions is the up-to-date cross-platform rollup in the meantime.
- The Executive Summary "Generate Summary" AI action recomputes Analytics independently of the dashboard's own already-computed data (a cross-request duplication, not a within-request one).
- 7 fully-built, authenticated Server Action files have zero production callers and are pending an explicit keep-or-delete decision.

Full detail, severity, and fix paths for every item above are in `docs/v1.0-known-limitations.md`.
